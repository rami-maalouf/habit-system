# coin casino (v1, physical)

variable-reward layer on top of [[night-shutdown]] and [[morning-ignition]]. source idea: "I built a habit system as addicting as a casino" (SpoonFedStudy, knowledge-base). deliberately stripped: one jar, one die, two coin types, two rails. the full wheel/tiers/bonus-round machinery is banned until this survives 30 days.

exact definitions of reps, the reward menu, and the redemption protocol live in [[rewards-and-coins]] - that file is the single source of truth when a "does this count?" question comes up.

## setup (once, ~$5)

- one clear jar on the desk, visible from the chair
- a bag of coins (or paper clips) next to it
- one six-sided die
- a note on the jar: "$1 per coin. fun money."

## earning coins (exactly three reps, nothing else)

1. **deep work coin**: morning ignition executed - first 10 minutes of the planned block started before any reactive inputs. one coin. this is the priority rep; the casino exists to pay out on the keystone habit.
2. **courage coin**: one discrete uncomfortable social action, completed. a brave ask, approaching a stranger, initiating a hard conversation, publishing something vulnerable. "i talked well in a meeting" does not count - it must be a single nameable act that cost comfort. max 2 courage coins per day so the jar stays honest.

3. **closed day coin**: shutdown sentence written AND in bed by 23:30. coin drops and rolls the next morning (see [[rewards-and-coins]] for the full definition).

no other reps in v1. not gym, not chores, not "being productive". if a rep feels arguable, it does not earn.

## the roll

right after earning a coin: drop it in the jar, roll the die.

youtube pays 2x feed time (feeds are the more corrosive channel, so they cost more per minute). the multiplier applies to small and medium wins only - the jackpot is flat, otherwise it becomes a 2-hour binge license.

- **1-3**: small win - 10 min feed, or 20 min youtube, or one planned snack
- **4-5**: medium win - 30 min feed or 60 min youtube
- **6**: jackpot - 60 minutes flat (either channel) plus one bonus coin straight into the jar

youtube redemptions always run with a timer started before the first video plays. no timer = it was naked. and reward youtube is entertainment by definition: a "productive" video does not upgrade the session into unlogged free time - genuine learning material goes to the knowledge-base watch-later pipeline instead.

wins are vouchers. redeem any time before the day closes, never inside a work block. unredeemed wins expire at "the day is closed" - they do not bank overnight.

## the naked rule (the load-bearing rule)

**youtube-for-entertainment and social feeds are never naked again.** they exist only as rolled rewards.

the work-youtube line, so there is no arguing with yourself at 4pm:
- **work youtube** = a specific video you can name, opened for a task you are currently doing, via search or a direct link. allowed.
- **reward youtube** = the homepage, recommendations, shorts, subscriptions feed, world cup clips, psychology videos, anything you would describe as "watching". rolled reward only.
- social media (instagram, x, linkedin) feeds: rolled reward only. posting and replying to dms is not gated.

snacks are not gated (they are an alternative prize, not a naked-rule target).

## the money

every coin = $1 earmarked to the fun-money fund. spend it guilt-free on anything: concerts, trips, gear. count the jar weekly, move the real dollars, empty nothing - the coins stay as the visible progress bar.

## the two rails

1. **the casino closes when the day closes.** no rolls, no redemptions after the shutdown sentence. intermittent reinforcement is the same mechanism as the old 1am loops - it does not get to run at that hour.
2. **if the tracking ever takes longer than the rep, simplify.** (source video's own rule, and the autopsy of every dead system before it.)

## banned until day 30

- the wheel, reward tiers, colored/golden clips, bonus rounds, near-miss mechanics, bonus-round countdown timers (the redemption timer is required, not banned)
- second and third jars
- any software: no widget, no convex table, no skill, no shortcuts automation. the intent-app integration is the day-31 build, designed from 30 days of lived data.
