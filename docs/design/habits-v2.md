# habits v2: the daily chain

v1 ([[night-shutdown]], [[morning-ignition]], [[coin-casino]]) survived past day 30. v2 exists because the system was "up in the air": habits were described, not defined. this file words every habit the atomic habits way - identity, implementation intention, stack, 2-minute version, four laws, one checkable line - so each one can be tracked and each check is a vote.

v1 files stay the source of truth for their rituals. this file defines the chain and the wording. [[rewards-and-coins]] stays the source of truth for coins.

## the identity

> i am in control of my day. i close it, i open it, it does not run me.

every checkbox below is one vote for that person. that is the whole tracking philosophy: the streak is not the point, the votes are.

## the architecture: one chain, not six habits

six separate habits die. one chain survives, because each habit is the cue for the next and prayer anchors both ends. nothing here needs a new alarm except the wake alarm that already exists.

isha / 22:30 alarm -> shutdown -> stage runway -> bed -> wake -> dump (speak, no input) -> fajr prayer -> ignition

---

## 1. night shutdown (the keystone, unchanged)

- **identity vote**: i decide tomorrow tonight.
- **implementation intention**: i will answer the three questions and write the shutdown sentence at isha or the 22:30 alarm, whichever comes first, at my desk.
- **stack**: after the isha adhan (or the alarm), i open tomorrow's daily note.
- **2-minute version**: on a dead night, the sentence alone counts. "tomorrow i go to the gym at 9" counts. after a zero day it counts double.
- **four laws**: obvious - adhan + alarm, two cues already firing daily. attractive - it is the ticket to the closed-day coin roll tomorrow morning. easy - three questions, five minutes, no ai. satisfying - say "the day is closed" out loud, check the box.
- **check**: `- [ ] closed: shutdown sentence written + "the day is closed" said, by 23:00`

## 2. day closed by 23:00, in bed by 23:30

how stopping before midnight becomes natural, not a rule: you cannot will a habit natural. it becomes natural when the cue fires the routine enough times without negotiation, and when the alternative stops paying. so v2 attacks late-night work with the inverted four laws instead of discipline:

- **invisible**: staging the runway closes every tab except tomorrow's first action. an empty desktop is a weak cue for "one more thing".
- **unattractive**: the casino closes at "the day is closed". work after close earns nothing - no coin, no roll, no credit. late work becomes unpaid work.
- **difficult**: laptop lid shut and phone charging out of arm's reach are part of staging the runway.
- **unsatisfying**: an open day at midnight means no closed-day coin rolls tomorrow, and the box stays unchecked in the note.

and the structural lever: the 6:30 wake (habit 3) makes a 1am night self-punishing within a week. the night ends because the morning matters. that is the "it just makes sense" you asked for - not a rule, a consequence.

- **identity vote**: my day has an edge. i am not negotiable after it.
- **implementation intention**: i will be in bed with the phone out of reach by 23:30 in my room.
- **stack**: after i say "the day is closed", i stage the runway, dim the lights, brush, and go to bed.
- **2-minute version**: lights dimmed and phone on the far charger. even if sleep comes late, the environment vote was cast.
- **check**: `- [ ] bed: in bed by 23:30, phone out of reach`

## 3. wake at the first alarm

- **identity vote**: i wake up in control. waking late tells my brain someone else runs the day.
- **implementation intention**: i will get out of bed within 5 minutes of the 6:30 alarm in my room, no snooze.
- **stack**: after the alarm rings, my feet hit the floor and i walk to the bathroom. the phone stays on its charger.
- **2-minute version**: feet on floor, out of the room. that alone checks the box on a brutal day.
- **four laws**: obvious - one alarm, same time, labeled with tomorrow's first action. attractive - the closed-day coin from last night rolls only after i am up. easy - it is downstream of habit 2; sleep pressure does the heavy lifting. satisfying - the roll, then the checkbox.
- **check**: `- [ ] wake: out of bed within 5 minutes of the alarm, no snooze`

## 4. the morning dump: output before input

your phrase, and it is the law of the morning: put out before you take in. speak before you read. nothing external gets a vote until you have voted first.

- **identity vote**: my first words each day are mine, not a feed's.
- **implementation intention**: i will speak my plan out loud in the bathroom immediately after waking, before touching any input.
- **stack**: after my feet hit the floor, i go to the bathroom and talk myself through the day: last night's shutdown sentence from memory, then the day skeleton - morning block, after lunch, evening, gym or no gym. then wudu and fajr prayer. then one water bottle.
- **the line that keeps this from becoming re-planning**: the needle-mover task is locked. it was chosen last night and does not get re-decided in the morning, ever - that is the drift vector that killed four routines. the dump only schedules around it. if there was no shutdown sentence last night, the dump is where the fallback sentence gets written, before the phone does anything else.
- **2-minute version**: say the shutdown sentence out loud once. that counts.
- **four laws**: obvious - the bathroom is the cue; you are going there anyway. attractive - it is speaking, which you like and are good at; the mirror ted talk you have wanted since 2023. easy - zero tools, zero apps, already awake. satisfying - you walk out already knowing the whole day; check the box after prayer.
- **check**: `- [ ] dump: spoke the plan out loud + prayed, before any input`

## 5. morning ignition (unchanged)

- **identity vote**: i am the person who starts.
- **implementation intention**: i will do the first 10 minutes of the planned block at its calendar time, at the staged desk.
- **stack**: after fajr prayer and water, i sit at the desk where exactly one thing is open: the first action.
- **2-minute version**: the first 10 minutes are already the floor. i only owe ten.
- **four laws**: as written in [[morning-ignition]] - staged runway, calendar block as alarm, no reactive inputs before ignition, intentionality tracker as referee. earns the deep work coin.
- **check**: `- [ ] ignition: first 10 minutes started before any reactive input`

## 6. courage rep (optional, unchanged)

one nameable uncomfortable social act. defined in [[coin-casino]]. optional daily, max 2 coins.

- **check**: `- [ ] courage: one nameable act that cost comfort`

## the daily note block

paste into the daily template, under its own heading:

```markdown
## chain
- [ ] closed: shutdown sentence written + "the day is closed" said, by 23:00
- [ ] bed: in bed by 23:30, phone out of reach
- [ ] wake: out of bed within 5 minutes of the alarm, no snooze
- [ ] dump: spoke the plan out loud + prayed, before any input
- [ ] ignition: first 10 minutes started before any reactive input
- [ ] courage: one nameable act that cost comfort (optional)
```

five required boxes. that is the whole system. if a box feels arguable, it is unchecked - same honesty rule as the jar.

## the rules

1. **never miss twice.** one unchecked box is a data point. the same box unchecked two days running is an emergency: fix the environment, not the willpower, before anything else that day.
2. **the weekly count is the only review.** at the friday jar count: count coins, count checks per box, apply never-miss-twice at the week level (any box under 4/7 gets one environment fix). wording changes to this file happen only here - same amendment rule as [[rewards-and-coins]].
3. **the 2-minute version always counts.** a checked floor beats an unchecked ceiling. bad days are legal; they are what the floors are for.
4. **if tracking takes longer than the rep, simplify.** carried over from v1. still the autopsy of every dead system before it.

## the app

the tracker app you want to build has its spec right here: five fixed boxes, one optional box, one screen, votes not streaks displayed (checks per box per week), never-miss-twice as the only alert. no paywall, no habit library, no configuration. it earns the right to exist after these boxes survive 30 days on paper - same gate the casino passed. until then the daily note is the tracker.
