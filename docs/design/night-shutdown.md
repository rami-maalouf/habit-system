# night shutdown

the one keystone habit. everything else in this system waits behind it. see [[The One Keystone Habit - Nightly Shutdown]] in the vault for the full evidence and reasoning.

## the trigger

isha, or a 10:30pm alarm, whichever comes first. the alarm is the whole enforcement mechanism. no app, no reminder skill, just the alarm.

## the ritual (5 minutes, done alone, no AI)

three questions, in this order. the order matters: selection and definition first, because at night i overthink and ruminate. justification is folded into question 1.

1. **which one task moves the needle tomorrow?**
   if i can't say why it beats every other task on the list, it's the wrong task. this one question absorbs all three of my old why-questions (why it matters, why now, which pillar). if the answer is hollow, that is the signal - pick something else.

2. **what does done look like?**
   one tangible outcome, sized to a single session. "work on the pipeline" is not done. "the youtube script is drafted and read out loud once" is done.

3. **when do i start, and what's the first 10 minutes?**
   the start time goes straight into tomorrow's calendar block. the first action has to be physical and specific enough to survive grogginess: "open workflow.py and reproduce the bug", not "look at the pipeline".

## the output

one line in tomorrow's daily note:

> tomorrow i [task] at [time]. done when [outcome]. starting with [first 10 minutes].

then, out loud: **"the day is closed."**

## the rules

- one task. a needle-mover, not clutter.
- after "the day is closed": no new work. no new tabs, tickets, tools, or skills. rest is allowed, work is not.
- bad days are legal. "tomorrow i go to the gym at 9" counts. writing it after a zero day counts double.
- missed a night: write it the next morning before the phone does anything else. it counts.
- no streaks, no scores, no dashboard. for the first 30 days this lives in the daily note and nowhere else.

## why the 6-question version is not here

the full 6 questions (why it matters / why now / which pillar / what really needs achieving / easiest way / how to make it fun) belong to daytime session-starts and weekly planning, which is what the task-framing skill is scoped for. that version runs with energy, out loud, with the skill. this night version is the depleted-state version: three questions i can answer tired, alone, in five minutes. six open questions at 10:30pm is a morning-sized ritual in the wrong window, and that is how the last four routines died.
