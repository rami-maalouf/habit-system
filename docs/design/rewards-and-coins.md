# rewards and coins: the ledger

the single source of truth for what a coin is, how one is earned, and exactly what a win buys. the point of this file: **zero decisions at redemption time.** if a question comes up mid-day ("does this count?", "can i watch this?"), the answer is already written here. if it isn't written here, the answer is no.

mechanics live in [[coin-casino]]. this file is only definitions and menus.

---

## the currency

- **1 coin = 1 completed rep = $1** into the fun-money fund.
- coins are physical. they enter the jar at the moment of the rep, never retroactively at night ("i think i earned three today" is worth zero).
- coins never leave the jar. the jar is the progress bar; the dollars move separately, counted once a week.
- there is exactly one currency. no multipliers on coins, no golden clips, no streak bonuses in v1.

## earning: the two reps, precisely

### 1. deep work coin (max 1/day)

**earned the moment** the first 10 minutes of the planned morning block are done, having started before any reactive input (dms, email, notifications, stocks, feeds).

- counts: started the block groggy, did only the 10 minutes, then fell apart. still a coin. the coin pays for ignition, not for the whole day.
- does not count: did 3 hours of great work in the afternoon after a phone-first morning. the coin is for the sequence, not the volume.
- does not count: no shutdown sentence was written last night, so there was no planned block. (the casino pays out on the keystone system only.)

### 2. courage coin (max 2/day)

**earned the moment** one discrete, nameable social action that cost comfort is completed.

- counts: approached a stranger, made a brave ask, initiated a hard conversation, asked for the thing directly instead of hinting, published something vulnerable, gave someone uncomfortable honest feedback kindly.
- does not count: "i was social today", "the meeting went well", "i almost approached", being friendly when it was easy.
- the test: could you write the act as one sentence starting with a verb, naming a person or audience? if not, no coin.

### 3. closed day coin (max 1/day, adopted 2026-07-08)

**earned when both halves of ending the day are done:**
- (a) the shutdown sentence is written in tomorrow's daily note, and
- (b) in bed, lights out, by **23:30** (adjust only at the weekly count).

the clock decides, not a "calmly" judgment. miss either half, no coin.

**mechanical twist:** the coin drops and rolls the NEXT MORNING, alongside the ignition roll. the casino stays closed at night, and feed minutes cannot be redeemed in bed. a fully closed day followed by a clean ignition = a two-coin, two-roll morning.

**nothing else earns.** not gym, not chores, not shipping code, not processing the vault. if a rep feels arguable, it does not earn - arguable coins are how the jar stops meaning anything.

## the reward menu

every win is one pick from the row you rolled. no combining rows, no splitting a win across channels.

| roll | win | option a: feed | option b: youtube | option c: snack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | small | 10 min | 20 min | one planned snack |
| 4-5 | medium | 30 min | 60 min | - |
| 6 | jackpot | 60 min flat, either channel, + 1 bonus coin into the jar | | |

- **feed** = instagram, x, linkedin scrolling. (posting and replying to dms are never gated.)
- **youtube** = entertainment youtube: homepage, recommendations, shorts, subscriptions, world cup clips, psychology videos, anything you would call "watching". pays 2x feed minutes because feeds are the more corrosive channel.
- **snack** = one pre-planned item from the snack list below, eaten because you chose it, not because it was in front of you.

### the snack list (pre-approved, edit only at the weekly count)

- one dessert item when out (single, not "dessert course")
- one chocolate bar / pastry at home
- one specialty drink (the fancy coffee, the mango juice)

anything not on this list is not a snack reward, it's just eating. the free-food rule from My Principles still applies on top: never from a plate holding more than the intended serving.

## redemption protocol

1. a win is a **voucher**: earned at roll time, redeemable any moment before the day closes, never inside a work block.
2. **timer before the first video/scroll.** phone timer, set to the exact minutes won, started before the content opens. no timer = the session was naked.
3. when the timer fires: finish the current video if under 2 minutes remain on it, otherwise close mid-video. feeds close immediately.
4. unredeemed vouchers **expire at "the day is closed."** no banking, no rollover, no doubling tomorrow.
5. one voucher active at a time. no stacking two wins into one long session.

## what the fund buys

fun money is guilt-free by definition: concerts, trips, gear, taking someone out. the only rule is it must be spent on wants, not needs - groceries laundered through the fund kill the reward value.

## amendment rule

this file changes only at the **weekly count** (when coins are tallied and dollars moved). mid-week rule inventions - new reps, new multipliers, new menu items - are automatically void, no matter how good they feel in the moment. write the idea at the bottom of this file under "proposed", decide it at the count.
